Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Deepstash has no account and no server. The items, photos and memories you save stay on your iPhone; we can't see or access them.

Barcode lookups

When a barcode is scanned, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, Open Library or Google Books to look up the product name. Only the number is sent, never photos or anything else.

AI

AI is off by default. With Apple built-in, processing happens on your iPhone without the network. With your own API key, the data needed is sent directly to the service you chose when you tap an AI button, and once a day for the home screen note; photos, prices, stores and memories are never sent. That service handles the data under its own privacy policy. The API key is stored only in this iPhone's keychain.

Camera, photos, notifications

The camera is used to photograph items and read barcodes and expiry dates, the photo library to pick photos, and notifications for expiry reminders. They are used only when you use or turn them on, and photos are kept inside the app.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads below Home, Spaces and Search. Ads are always non-personalized; the app never asks for tracking permission and never gives your items, photos or memories to advertisers. To serve and measure ads, Google receives device data and approximate location, handled under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland first see Google's consent form and can change their choice under Ad privacy options in Settings. Pro stops loading ads.

Payments

Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Voice

When you hold to talk to Lumi, the iPhone's speech recognition turns your voice into text. On phones that support on-device recognition nothing is sent out; otherwise Apple's speech recognition service handles it. We never receive your voice.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies to the website only: the app does not include Google Analytics. You can block it in your browser's privacy settings or with Google's opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Items deleted in the app are permanently removed after 30 days.
