Support

Questions or ideas? Write to sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com.

Does it cost anything?

The free version has every feature, up to 3 places (areas and groups don't count), the 2 most recent chats with Lumi, and one banner ad below Home, Spaces and Search. Pro removes both limits and the ads: US$1 a week, US$3 a month, or US$5 once (local prices are shown in the App Store).

Can I lose my data?

Your data lives in the app on this iPhone and is included in your iPhone backups. You can also export a CSV from Settings (the gear at the top right of Home). There is no iCloud sync yet.

Moving to a new iPhone?

Use iPhone backup and restore.

A barcode isn't found?

The barcode databases don't have every product, especially small local brands. Give it a name yourself; the next scan of that barcode uses your name.

The expiry date was read wrong?

A read date is marked "please check" and you can change it before saving.

How do I turn on AI?

Go to Settings (the gear on Home) › AI and choose Apple built-in or paste your own API key. The app works fully without it.

What does AI see?

When you tap an AI button, only what that feature needs is sent. "Look at this place" sends item names, amounts, expiry dates and categories. Once AI is on, the daily note on the home screen sends a short summary of your home (item names, counts and dates) once a day. Neither sends photos, prices, stores or memories.

Deleted something by mistake?

Settings (the gear on Home) › Recently Deleted keeps it for 30 days.

What is an 800 Closet?

A walk-in storage closet about 80 cm deep, often next to the entrance or a hallway, for household supplies, suitcases and seasonal things.
